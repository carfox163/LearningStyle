What Are Learning Styles? 什么是学习风格？

Most people do not belong to only one single learning style. We usually have a mix of several styles, with one or two being dominant. Knowing your learning preference helps you choose better study strategies.

大多数人不属于单一的学习风格。我们通常是多种风格的混合体，其中一两种占主导。了解自己的学习偏好，可以帮你选择更适合自己的学习策略。

1. Visual 视觉型

You prefer using pictures, images, and spatial understanding.
你更喜欢借助图片、图像和空间感知来理解知识。

Visual learners absorb information best when they can see diagrams, charts, videos, mind maps or written notes. They think in pictures, and they often benefit from color‑coding notes, watching demonstrations, or reading textbooks with plenty of illustrations.

视觉型学习者在看到图表、曲线图、视频、思维导图或者书面笔记时吸收信息效果最好。他们以图像方式思考，用不同颜色标记笔记、观看演示、阅读带大量插图的教材，通常会让学习事半功倍。

2. Musical/Auditory 音乐 / 听觉型

You prefer using sound and music.
你更喜欢依靠声音与音乐来学习。

Auditory learners learn well through listening. Lectures, discussions, podcasts, audio books, rhymes and songs help them remember content. They can easily recall information they have heard, and they often benefit from reading aloud or explaining ideas verbally.

听觉型学习者通过听来高效学习。讲座、讨论、播客、有声书、韵律歌谣都能帮助他们记忆内容。他们很容易回忆起听过的信息，大声朗读、口头复述知识点对他们很有帮助。

3. Verbal 语言 / 言语型

You prefer using words, both in speech and writing.
你更喜欢运用文字，包括口头表达和书面写作。

Verbal learners love language. They enjoy reading, writing, storytelling, debates and word games. They master new knowledge best by describing concepts in words, writing summaries, or discussing ideas.

言语型学习者热爱语言。他们喜欢阅读、写作、讲故事、辩论和文字游戏。用文字描述概念、撰写总结、交流探讨观点，是他们掌握新知识最好的方式。

4. Physical/Kinesthetic 身体 / 动觉型

You prefer using your body, hands and sense of touch.
你更喜欢调动身体、双手以及触觉来学习。

Kinesthetic learners are hands‑on learners. They learn best through physical activities, experiments, movement and touching real objects. Sitting still for long periods is hard for them; they retain more knowledge when doing practical tasks, building models or field work.

动觉型学习者属于实操型学习者。通过肢体活动、实验、动手操作、触摸实物，他们的学习效果最好。长时间静坐对他们比较困难；实操任务、搭建模型、实地实践能让他们记住更多知识。

5. Logical/Mathematical 逻辑 / 数理型

You prefer using logic, reasoning and systems.
你更喜欢使用逻辑、推理与体系化的方式学习。

Logical learners like patterns, numbers, categories and clear rules. They enjoy puzzles, problem‑solving and exploring the links between ideas. They ask lots of “why” questions and prefer to break complex problems down step by step.

逻辑型学习者喜欢规律、数字、分类和清晰的规则。他们喜爱解谜、解决问题、探究观点之间的关联。他们会不断追问 “为什么”，习惯一步步拆解复杂问题。

6. Social 社交型

You prefer to learn in groups or with other people.
你更喜欢小组学习，和其他人一起学习。

Social learners thrive in teamwork, group discussions and peer cooperation. They gain new insights by talking with classmates, listening to different opinions and working together on projects. They are good at communicating and understanding others’ perspectives.

社交型学习者在团队协作、小组讨论中学习状态最好。通过和同学交流、倾听不同观点、合作完成项目，他们能获得新理解。他们擅长沟通，容易理解他人的想法。

7. Solitary 独立 / 独处型

You prefer to work alone and use self‑study.
你更喜欢独自学习，采用自学的方式。

Solitary learners are self‑directed. They concentrate best when studying by themselves, set personal learning goals and reflect quietly. They enjoy self‑paced reading and independent research, and they understand their own learning needs clearly.

独处型学习者自主性强。独自学习时专注力最高，能够自主设定学习目标、安静思考。他们适合自主阅读、独立探究，很清楚自己的学习需求。
